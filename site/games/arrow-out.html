About this game

Arrow Out is built around simple visual logic. The objective is to clear the board by choosing the right arrow order and opening routes without trapping yourself.

It works well as a browser game because the rules are easy to learn, rounds are short, and failure is quick enough that replay remains satisfying.

How to Play

  1. Start the level and inspect how arrows are blocked.
  2. Tap or click arrows that can safely move out first.
  3. Open paths for the remaining arrows by removing blockers.
  4. Clear the whole board to complete the level.

Controls

  • Mouse: Click to select an arrow
  • Touch: Tap to remove or move an arrow
  • Mobile: Fully playable in short portrait sessions
