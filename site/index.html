Why this direction

Arrow Out is not the biggest puzzle keyword cluster, but it is a realistic first-site validation target. The demand is strong enough to matter and lighter than highly saturated block puzzle head terms.

This site is intentionally small. The goal is to ship, get indexed, and learn which page model responds first.
